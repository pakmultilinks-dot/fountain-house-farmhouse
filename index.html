The Estate

A rare corner estate in the heart of Lahore's farmhouse belt

The Fountain House sits on 25.5 kanal of manicured land in UK Farms, Hudiara, on Barki Road, Lahore. A 3-sided corner plot wrapped in a solid boundary wall with wall-height plantations for complete privacy.

At its centre: a 4,000 sqft modern residence, a three-tier stone fountain on a circular courtyard, a 37-ft swimming pool with 12-ft jacuzzi, lush lawns, a walking track and a separate vegetation area. Power is fully secured with 10 kW solar, a 25 kVA transformer and 16 CCTV cameras.
