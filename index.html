The Estate

A rare corner estate in the heart of Lahore's farmhouse belt

The Fountain House sits on 25.5 kanal of manicured land in UK Farms, Hudiara, on Barki Road, Lahore. The plot is a 3-sided corner, wrapped in a solid boundary wall with wall-height plantations that keep the estate completely private.

At its centre stands a 4,000 sqft modern residence with double-glazed windows, marble floors and cove-lit ceilings. A tiled driveway leads to a three-tier stone fountain on a circular courtyard, the signature view of the house.

The grounds are built for year-round living and entertaining: a 37-ft swimming pool with a 12-ft jacuzzi, lush lawns, a private walking track, a separate vegetation area, two water bores and full staff accommodation. Power is fully secured with 10 kW solar (16 kW approved load, Huawei 12 kW inverter with 6 kVA hybrid), a 25 kVA transformer and 16 CCTV cameras watching the perimeter.
